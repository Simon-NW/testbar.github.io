I am currently pursuing a M.Sc. in Computer Science at the University of Copenhagen and am a Student Researcher in Mikkel Abrahamsen's group at BARC (Basic Algorithm Research Copenhagen). Before that, I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science at the University of Bonn. My main research interests are algorithms and complexity, specifically approximation algorithms and computational geometry. I am supported by the Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes scholarship.
